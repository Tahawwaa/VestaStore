# Local fashion imagery

All ten final images were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project, then encoded as optimized progressive JPEGs without changing their composition. The original PNGs remain in the tool's generated-image directory. No CLI image-generation fallback was used. Unsplash and Pexels could not be reached from the development environment, so the finished app deliberately has no external photography dependency.

Final files: `public/images/hero.jpg`, `blazer.jpg`, `shirt.jpg`, `bag.jpg`, `sneaker.jpg`, `knit.jpg`, `coat.jpg`, `trousers.jpg`, `scarf.jpg`, and `category-men.jpg`.

The hero is also used for the women's category. The men's editorial image is reused for the promotional story. Product close-ups are CSS crops of the original photos, so fabric, buttons, and proportions remain consistent.

## Hero prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium editorial fashion campaign photograph for a Persian minimalist fashion brand. Portrait 4:5 composition. An elegant adult Persian woman with dark hair wearing an oversized oatmeal linen blazer over an ivory shirt and taupe wide-leg trousers, walking beside warm weathered beige stone architecture in soft autumn daylight. Cropped editorial composition from lips down to knees with focus on beautiful natural linen textures and relaxed tailoring; dark brown leather bag in hand. Quiet luxury, muted earthy colors, soft natural shadows, realistic film photography, shot on medium format. No text, no typography, no logos, no collage, no watermark. Beautiful premium magazine photograph, detailed fabric, plausible anatomy.
